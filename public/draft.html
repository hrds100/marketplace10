⚠️ One Thing to Know Upfront

Around 70% of property agreements are on rolling month-to-month contracts with landlords — not fixed long-term leases. The landlord relationships are strong and established, but a buyer should plan to convert these to fixed leases after acquisition. This is already reflected in the asking price — a fully secured portfolio of this size would be worth significantly more.
